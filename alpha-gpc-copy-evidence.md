# Alpha-GPC Formula and Copy Decisions

The user confirmed the stick-pack formula provides **500 mg of a 50% Alpha-GPC material per serving**. Customer-facing dose labels should therefore read **Alpha-GPC (50%) — 500 mg** and should not describe the serving as 500 mg of active Alpha-GPC.

For immediate-effect copy, a 2024 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial in 20 healthy men found improved Stroop performance 60 minutes after 315 mg or 630 mg Alpha-GPC, while Flanker and N-Back outcomes did not significantly differ. The page should use qualified language such as “supports the acetylcholine pathway used for attention and memory” rather than promising an immediate memory effect.[1]

For consistent-use copy, a 2021 single-blind randomized placebo-controlled study in 39 healthy adults found a trend toward improved motivation across two weeks and significantly higher nighttime motivation, with no effect on anxiety. The page should describe this as emerging support for motivation rather than a guaranteed outcome.[2]

A 2017 randomized study in 48 healthy men found that 250 mg and 500 mg Alpha-GPC increased serum free choline but did not improve psychomotor vigilance. This is useful as transparent mechanism evidence and supports avoiding exaggerated reaction-time claims.[3]
