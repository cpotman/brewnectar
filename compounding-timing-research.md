# Compounding Effect Timing Research

The proposed sequence is **Day 1 — L-Theanine, Alpha-GPC, B6 + B12; Week 2 — Prebiotic + Probiotic; Week 6 — Rhodiola + Cordyceps; Week 12 — Ashwagandha + Lion's Mane**. The milestone copy must describe when the referenced human research assessed outcomes, not promise that every customer will feel an effect on that date.

Alpha-GPC was assessed 60 minutes after ingestion in a randomized crossover trial of 20 healthy men. The 315 mg and 630 mg groups improved Stroop total-score changes versus placebo, while not every cognitive measure improved. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39683633/

L-Theanine has both acute human research and a four-week randomized crossover study. In the latter, 200 mg/day was associated with improvements in selected stress, sleep, verbal-fluency, and executive-function measures, although between-condition cognitive differences were limited. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6836118/

Rhodiola was assessed after 28 days in a randomized placebo-controlled trial of 60 adults with stress-related fatigue. Compared with placebo, the standardized extract improved burnout and selected attention measures. Week 6 is therefore a conservative page milestone rather than an onset promise. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19016404/

The current Cordyceps URL in `StickPack.tsx` is mismatched: PMID 27736246 is a green-tea and sour-tea trial, not a Cordyceps study. The correct Hirsch et al. trial assessed a 4 g/day multi-mushroom blend containing *Cordyceps militaris*. One week produced limited between-group effects; an exploratory three-week subgroup showed improved VO2max and possible improvements in other exercise-tolerance measures. The blend, dose, and outcomes differ from BrewNectar, so Week 6 should be described as a conservative multi-week checkpoint rather than a guaranteed Cordyceps onset. Correct source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5236007/

B6 and B12 belong on Day 1 as ongoing nutritional cofactors that contribute to normal energy metabolism and nervous-system function, not as ingredients claimed to create an immediate perceptible effect. The Prebiotic + Probiotic blend belongs at Week 2 as the beginning of consistent gut support; the linked human gut–brain trials assessed outcomes over four to twelve weeks, so the copy should call Week 2 a build phase. Ashwagandha and Lion's Mane remain at Week 12 because the linked human studies generally use multi-week windows, including roughly eight to sixteen weeks, rather than an acute assessment.
