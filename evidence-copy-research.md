# Stick-Pack Evidence Copy Research

The Evidence cards will replace the metaphorical “clock” labels with concise **Study Timing** labels. The right-side callouts will summarize outcomes actually measured in the linked research rather than restating product dose, Daily Value, or unsupported consumer timing.

| Ingredient | Study timing | Benefit callout | Evidence basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha-GPC | 60-minute assessment | Both tested doses improved Stroop scores versus placebo in healthy men | A randomized crossover trial assessed cognition 60 minutes after 315 mg or 630 mg Alpha-GPC. Both doses improved Stroop total-score change versus placebo; other cognitive measures did not consistently improve.[1] |
| L-Theanine | 4-week trial | Verbal fluency and executive-function scores improved | A randomized crossover trial of 200 mg/day in 30 healthy adults reported improvements in verbal fluency and executive-function scores after four weeks, with important limitations in between-condition comparisons.[2] |
| Lion’s Mane | 28 days–16 weeks | Cognition and subjective stress improved in a 28-day healthy-adult study | The current linked research includes a 28-day healthy-adult study and a 16-week mild-cognitive-impairment study; the callout will use the healthy-adult cognition and subjective-stress outcomes already described in the card.[3] |
| Rhodiola rosea | 28-day trial | Fatigue and several attention measures improved versus placebo | A 60-person randomized placebo-controlled trial used 576 mg/day for 28 days and reported significant treatment effects for burnout/fatigue and three computerized attention indices.[4] |
| Ashwagandha | 10-week trial | Sleep efficiency reached 83.5% | Existing card language accurately summarizes its linked randomized trial and will remain.[5] |
| Cordyceps | 3-week trial | VO2max improved 10.9% | Existing card language accurately summarizes its linked trial and will remain.[6] |
| Prebiotic Fiber + Probiotic | 2-week human trial | Higher Bifidobacteria versus placebo at the studied 5–8 g/day doses | A double-blind crossover study in 30 healthy adults found significant bifidogenic effects after two weeks at both 5 g/day and 8 g/day inulin. The Evidence card explicitly displays the studied dose range so it is not confused with the product’s 2 g dose.[7] |
| Vitamins B6 + B12 | Mechanism review | Foundational roles in cellular energy and neurochemical synthesis | The linked review supports essential coenzyme roles in cellular energy production and synthesis of neurochemicals, but it does not establish a consumer-perceived outcome at 100% Daily Value. The card therefore frames this as **Mechanism review** background rather than a trial-derived benefit statistic.[8] |

The currently linked probiotic and inulin PubMed identifiers are unrelated articles. They will be corrected to the actual _Bacillus coagulans_ in-vitro paper and the Kolida human inulin trial while preserving clear distinctions between laboratory survival data and human outcomes.[7] [9]
