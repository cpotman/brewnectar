# Dynamic Ship-By Reference

The referenced BioRoot product page places a compact shipment promise directly below the key product benefits and immediately above the purchase options. It uses a small green status dot followed by the concise label **“Ships by Thu, Aug 27”**, with the date emphasized, alongside a secondary fast-shipping note.

For BrewNectar’s existing green stock callout, retain the current integrated visual treatment and replace **“Available for Next-Day Dispatch”** with **“Order now and ships by: [date]”**. The date will be calculated once per browsing session as the next business day, skipping Saturday and Sunday, and formatted as `Thu, Aug 27`.
