# Ingredient Visual Reference

The established ingredient artwork is square, text-free, warm editorial macro photography with shallow depth of field. The palette uses cream, honey, amber, soft brown, and selective natural greens. Subjects are centered or slightly offset, fill most of the lower two-thirds, and sit against softly blurred backgrounds with glowing highlights. The imagery should feel premium, tactile, natural, and consistent as a carousel set rather than clinical stock photography.

The Citicoline visual uses a translucent amber brain model with warm internal pinpoints and cream bokeh. The L-Theanine visual uses dew-covered green tea leaves on a cream ceramic surface with soft golden daylight. New visuals should retain the same warm exposure, square crop, gentle bokeh, crisp foreground texture, and absence of text, labels, containers, or product packaging.

The Lion’s Mane artwork is an extreme macro crop that emphasizes the mushroom’s cascading white texture against an amber-to-cream background. The B-vitamin artwork uses a top-down editorial still life on cream ceramic with citrus rounds, small powder piles, soft shadows, and warm directional light. Across the set, compositions can vary between macro profile and restrained top-down still life, but must retain tactile realism, clean cream surfaces, amber warmth, and a single unmistakable ingredient story.
